Physiology

Fluid Balance & Cellular Osmolality

Water serves as the biological solvent for all metabolic enzymes, maintains plasma volume, lubricates articular joints, and moderates thermoregulation via evaporative perspiration.

A body mass loss of just 1% to 2% from dehydration impairs cognitive focus, aerobic performance, and subjective feelings of fatigue. Urine color (pale straw vs. dark amber) offers a simple, accessible qualitative biomarker of daily hydration status.

Safety Guidelines

Important Clinical Nuances (Hyponatremia)

Avoid Overhydration: Excessively consuming plain water rapidly without electrolytes during prolonged endurance exertion can induce exercise-associated hyponatremia (dangerously diluted serum sodium levels).

Renal & Cardiac Restrictions: Individuals with congestive heart failure, chronic kidney disease, or specific endocrine conditions must strictly follow physician-directed fluid restrictions rather than generalized formula estimates.
